These Shipping Terms set out the conditions under which Shak Distributors ships goods to customers in the United Kingdom and, where offered, internationally. They should be read alongside our Delivery Policy and Refund Policy.

1. Shipping coverage

  • We ship to addresses across mainland UK, Northern Ireland and UK islands.
  • International shipping may be available on request for trade orders — please contact us before ordering.

2. Shipping charges

  • Shipping charges are calculated and displayed at checkout based on the service selected, order weight and destination.
  • Standard UK delivery is free on orders over £150. Express and next-day services are charged as shown at checkout.

3. Dispatch & transit times

  • Orders are dispatched within 1 working day. Transit times (48-hour, 3–5 working day, etc.) begin after dispatch.
  • Estimated delivery times are guidelines, not guarantees, and may be affected by courier delays, weather or events outside our control.

4. Risk & title

  • Risk in the goods passes to you on delivery to the address provided.
  • Title (ownership) in the goods passes to you once we have received payment in full.

5. Inspection on delivery

Please inspect your parcel on arrival. If packaging is visibly damaged, note this with the courier where possible and contact us within 48 hours of delivery so we can resolve it quickly.

6. Customs, duties & taxes (international)

For any international shipments, the recipient is responsible for all import duties, taxes and customs clearance fees. These are not included in our prices or shipping charges.

7. Wholesale shipments

  • Bulk and pallet orders may carry bespoke shipping arrangements agreed in advance.
  • Trade customers must check quantities and IMEI/serial details against the delivery note on receipt and report discrepancies within 48 hours.

8. Lost or undelivered shipments

If tracking shows no movement or a parcel is confirmed lost, contact us and we will open a claim with the courier. Replacements or refunds are issued in line with our Refund Policy once the claim is resolved.

9. Contact

Questions about shipping? Reach us through our contact page.
