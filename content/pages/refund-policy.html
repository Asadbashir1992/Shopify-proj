At Shak Distributors we want you to be completely happy with your purchase. This Refund & Returns Policy explains your rights and how to return an item. It does not affect your statutory rights under UK consumer law.

1. Your 14-day right to cancel (consumers)

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, if you are a consumer buying online you may cancel your order within 14 days of receiving the goods, for any reason. You then have a further 14 days to return the item.

  • Items must be returned unused, in their original packaging and complete with all accessories.
  • Refunds for cancelled orders are issued within 14 days of us receiving the goods back (or proof of return).

2. Faulty or not-as-described items

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. If an item is faulty:

  • Within 30 days — you are entitled to a full refund.
  • Within 6 months — we will repair or replace the item; if that is not possible you may be entitled to a refund.

3. How to start a return

  1. Contact us via our contact page with your order number and reason for return.
  2. We will provide return instructions and a return address.
  3. Package the item securely and obtain proof of postage.

4. Return shipping costs

  • Faulty / incorrect items: we cover the cost of return.
  • Change of mind: the return postage is paid by you unless stated otherwise.

5. Refunds

  • Refunds are made to your original payment method.
  • Please allow up to 5–10 working days for the refund to appear after it is processed.
  • We may reduce a refund to reflect any loss in value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to check the goods.

6. Non-returnable items

For hygiene and safety reasons we cannot accept returns of certain items once opened (e.g. in-ear headphones with broken seals), unless faulty. Software, activation codes and gift cards are non-refundable.

7. Wholesale & trade orders

Business-to-business sales are not covered by consumer cancellation rights. Trade returns are accepted only for faulty goods or as agreed in your trade terms. Faulty trade units must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with IMEI/serial details.

8. Warranty

Devices are covered by the manufacturer’s warranty. We will help facilitate warranty claims where applicable.

9. Contact

Need help with a return? Get in touch through our contact page and we’ll sort it quickly.
