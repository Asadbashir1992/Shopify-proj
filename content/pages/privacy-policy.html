This Privacy Policy explains how Shak Distributors (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses and protects your personal data when you visit our store or place an order. We comply with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

Shak Distributors is the data controller for the personal data you provide. For any privacy questions, contact us via our contact page.

2. Information we collect

  • Order & account data: name, billing/delivery address, email, phone number and order history.
  • Payment data: processed securely by our payment providers (e.g. Shopify Payments, PayPal). We do not store full card details.
  • Trade account data: business name, VAT number and contact details where you apply for a wholesale account.
  • Technical data: IP address, device/browser type and cookie data collected automatically.

3. How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders and provide customer support.
  • To manage your account and (where applicable) your trade pricing.
  • To send order updates and, with your consent, marketing emails.
  • To prevent fraud and keep our store secure.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

4. Legal bases for processing

We rely on: contract (to fulfil your order), legal obligation (e.g. tax records), consent (marketing) and legitimate interests (to run and improve our business securely).

5. Sharing your information

We share data only with parties that help us run the store, including:

  • Shopify (our e-commerce platform);
  • payment processors;
  • couriers and fulfilment partners (to deliver your order);
  • professional advisers and authorities where legally required.

We never sell your personal data.

6. Cookies

We use cookies to keep your basket working, remember preferences and understand site usage. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect site functionality.

7. Data retention

We keep order and accounting records for as long as required by law (generally 6 years), and other data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us via our contact page.

9. Security

We use encryption (SSL) and reputable processors to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take appropriate measures to safeguard your information.

10. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle your data, you may contact the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
