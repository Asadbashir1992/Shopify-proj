This Delivery Policy explains how and when Shak Distributors dispatches and delivers your order. By placing an order you agree to the terms below.

Order processing & dispatch

  • Orders are dispatched within 1 working day. Orders placed before 3:00pm (Mon–Fri, excluding bank holidays) are normally dispatched the same or next working day.
  • Orders placed after 3pm, at weekends or on bank holidays are processed on the next working day.
  • You will receive a confirmation email, followed by a dispatch email with tracking once your parcel is on its way.

Delivery options & estimated times

Delivery time is in addition to the 1 working-day dispatch above. Choose your preferred option at checkout:

Tracking your order

Once dispatched you will receive a tracking number by email. Please allow a few hours for tracking to update after dispatch.

Wholesale & bulk orders

Large trade orders may be sent on a pallet or split across multiple parcels. We will agree a delivery schedule and any applicable charges with you before dispatch.

Failed deliveries & incorrect addresses

Please ensure your delivery address is correct at checkout. We cannot be held responsible for delays or losses caused by an incorrect or incomplete address. Re-delivery of returned-to-sender parcels may incur an additional charge.

Missing or delayed parcels

If your order has not arrived within the estimated time, contact us via our contact page with your order number and we will investigate with the courier right away.
